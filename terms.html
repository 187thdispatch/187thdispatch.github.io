The 187th Dispatch

Terms of Service · Updated November 12, 2026

What it is

The 187th Dispatch is a free weekly newspaper for the members of the [SUP] 187th Jet Corps community on Discord. It opens as a Discord Activity, a window inside Discord, and as a web page. All it does is show the paper.

Using it

You may read the paper and share it with the community. Don't use it to harass anyone, and follow Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines, which apply whenever you use Discord.

What's in it

The paper reports on the community's own week: events and training, promotions, honours, and photographs that members posted in the community's Discord server. The battalion's staff put it together with the help of software, so it can contain mistakes. It is not an official publication of Discord or of any game server.

No guarantees

The paper is provided as it is, without any warranty. It may change, pause or stop at any time.

Taking something out

If you want something removed, such as your name or a photo of you, ask a battalion officer on the community's Discord server. It will be taken out of the published page and left out of future editions.

Changes

These terms may be updated; the date above shows when they last changed.

Contact

Reach the paper's staff through the [SUP] 187th Jet Corps Discord server.
