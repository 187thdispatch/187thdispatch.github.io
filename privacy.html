The 187th Dispatch

Privacy Policy · Updated November 12, 2026

What the Activity collects

Nothing. The paper is a fixed page. It doesn't ask Discord for your account details, sets no cookies, and has no analytics, advertising or tracking. It doesn't send anything about you anywhere.

What stays on your device

If you choose a way to read (scrolling or turning pages), your browser remembers that choice in its local storage on your own device, so the paper opens the same way next time. Nothing else is stored.

Discord and GitHub

When you open the Activity, Discord loads the page through its own servers and gives it technical details such as a session identifier; the page doesn't keep them. What Discord does is covered by Discord's Privacy Policy. The page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep ordinary server logs, such as IP addresses, for security; see GitHub's Privacy Statement.

What the paper shows

The paper reports on the community: members' Discord or in-game names, ranks and roles, event results, and screenshots that members posted in the community's Discord server. It never includes private messages, email addresses or other contact details.

Your choices

To have your name or a photo left out, ask a battalion officer on the community's Discord server. It will be taken out of the published page and left out of future editions.

Age

The paper is for members of the Discord community, who must meet Discord's minimum age.

Changes and contact

If anything here changes, this page and its date will be updated. Reach the paper's staff through the [SUP] 187th Jet Corps Discord server.
